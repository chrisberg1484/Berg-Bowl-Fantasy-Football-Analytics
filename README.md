# Berg Bowl Fantasy Football Analytics

### Product Case Study | Fantasy Sports Analytics | Product Thinking

A data-driven fantasy football analytics project that transforms historical
auction, roster, and league performance data into actionable draft and
team-building insights.

---

## Overview

The Berg Bowl Fantasy Football Analytics project analyzes three seasons of
historical fantasy football data to identify patterns in auction strategy,
player valuation, positional spending, manager behavior, and team performance.

The project was developed as an independent product and analytics case study
to explore how historical fantasy data could be transformed into a
decision-support product for fantasy football managers.

---

## The Problem

Fantasy football managers have access to large amounts of player and league
data, but historical league-specific information is often difficult to
translate into actionable decisions.


---

## Objectives

- Analyze historical auction behavior across multiple seasons
- Identify positional spending patterns
- Evaluate player auction values and historical trends
- Compare manager drafting behavior
- Examine the relationship between draft strategy and season outcomes
- Identify opportunities for a fantasy football decision-support product

---

## Data

The analysis uses three seasons of Berg Bowl historical league data:

- 2023
- 2024
- 2025

The dataset includes:

- Player auction results
- Auction prices
- Player positions
- Drafting managers
- Final league standings
- Points For (PF)
- Championship outcomes

## Approach

### 1. Historical Auction Analysis

Analyzed auction prices by:

- Player
- Position
- Season
- Manager
- Spending distribution

### 2. Manager Behavior

Examined differences in drafting strategies between managers and
identified recurring spending patterns.

### 3. Performance Analysis

Compared draft behavior with:

- Final standings
- Points For
- Championship results

### 4. Product Opportunity

Used the analysis to identify how league-specific historical data could
be incorporated into a fantasy football decision-support experience.

---

## Key Insights

### Auction Strategy

Historical auction data can reveal how managers allocate limited draft
budgets across positions and players.

### Manager Strategy

Managers demonstrate identifiable drafting tendencies that can be analyzed
over multiple seasons.

### League-Specific Intelligence

Generic fantasy rankings do not necessarily capture the behavior of a
specific league.

Historical league data creates an opportunity to build recommendations
around the actual tendencies of a user's league.

### Product Opportunity

A fantasy football platform could combine historical league behavior with
player projections and auction values to provide personalized draft
recommendations.

---

## Product Concept

### League-Specific Fantasy Draft Intelligence

The next evolution of the analysis would be a product that answers questions
such as:

- What positions does my league typically overspend on?
- Which players are historically undervalued in this league?
- How much should I expect to pay for a player?
- Which managers consistently target certain positions?
- What draft strategies have historically produced successful teams?
- How does my current draft compare with historical championship teams?

The goal would be to move from:

**Historical Data → Analysis → Insight → Recommendation**

### Draft Recommendation System

roster construction, and historical league behavior.

A conversational interface allowing managers to ask questions about
